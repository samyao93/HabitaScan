The filter message

It asks nothing. It simply states the normal rules in Québec: visit first, first month's rent paid when the lease is signed. Any honest landlord accepts this, and it's exactly what a scammer wants to avoid.

In Québec, requiring a security deposit is illegal: only the first month's rent may be required in advance. Québec.ca (French)

Common rental scams in Québec

Fictional example

The landlord "abroad"

A great apartment at a low price. The owner is "on a mission overseas" and will mail the keys once you send a deposit by Interac. There is no apartment for you.

Fictional example

The rushed "deposit to hold it"

"Many people are interested, send $500 today to hold it." In Québec, no deposit can be required: only the first month's rent, when the lease is signed.

Fictional example

The fake lease transfer

Someone posing as the current tenant asks for "transfer fees". A real transfer needs the landlord's written consent, and the outgoing tenant can't ask you for anything in return.

Victim of a scam? Report it to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre: 1-888-495-8501.
